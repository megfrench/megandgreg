Our Expertise

Built for Federal Compliance & Scale

MEG AND GREG GROUP LLC operates a diversified portfolio of services designed to meet the rigorous demands of the U.S. Federal Government and its allied commercial partners. From intricate genomic research pipelines to massive logistical deployments, our central holding structure ensures unified compliance, financial stability, and operational excellence.

Prime Federal Contracting

As a registered WOSB entity, we are fully equipped to navigate complex solicitations, execute teaming agreements, and manage the complete lifecycle of federal contracts. Our deep understanding of the FAR and DFARS ensures zero-defect compliance from proposal to closeout.

  • Contract Management: End-to-end administration of Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) and IDIQ contracts.
  • Teaming & Subcontracting: Structuring compliant Joint Ventures and Mentor-Protégé agreements to maximize capability matrixes.
  • WAWF Mastery: Seamless invoicing and financial reporting through the Wide Area Workflow system.

DoD Logistics Brokerage

We act as a critical liaison in the supply chain, identifying bottlenecks and deploying domestic resources to ensure the warfighter and supporting agencies have exactly what they need, exactly when they need it.

  • Supply Chain Resilience: Vetting and securing domestic manufacturers to reduce reliance on foreign entities.
  • Vendor Management: Acting as the single point of contact between government buyers and networks of specialized subcontractors.
  • Rapid Deployment Fulfillment: Agile sourcing strategies for emergency response and emergent requirements.
