MEG AND GREG GROUP LLC is more than a holding company. We are a dedicated partner in federal contracting, domestic manufacturing, and veteran empowerment.

Our Foundation

Strategic Agility in the Federal Space

Founded on principles of operational integrity and unwavering compliance, MEG AND GREG GROUP LLC serves as the central nervous system for a growing portfolio of specialized brands and logistics operations. As a Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB), we bring diversity of thought and disciplined execution to every contract we manage.

The Meaning Behind the Name

M.E.G. Medical Emergency Governance

G.R.E.G. Government Relocation Efforts Guard

Our holding company structure provides us with unique agility. We centralize compliance, capital allocation, and strategic oversight, allowing our specialized divisions—like Lodging Sources and PINK PILLED—to focus entirely on mission execution for the Department of Defense, NIH, DHA, and other critical agencies.

Whether we are securing emergency housing for military personnel, brokering complex supply chain logistics, or incubating health science technologies, our operational tempo is unmatched.

Our Core Mission

"Keeping manufacturing and jobs in America while partnering with other businesses to provide preferential hiring to veterans."

Domestic Resilience

We believe that a strong national defense relies on a robust domestic supply chain. Through our logistics brokerage and strategic partnerships, we actively prioritize sourcing, manufacturing, and operational fulfillment within the United States, fortifying the industrial base.

Veteran Empowerment

Those who served our nation deserve our utmost support in the civilian workforce. We mandate preferential hiring for veterans across our holding company and strongly encourage the same commitment from our network of subcontractors and partners.
